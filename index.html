Are your bowls vegetarian?

Yes. AAHAR focuses on freshly prepared vegetarian protein bowls.

Can I order just one bowl?

Absolutely. The Paneer Protein Bowl is ₹159 and the Mexican Power Bowl is ₹169, plus the advertised ₹20 delivery charge where applicable.

What is included in the weekly and monthly plans?

Weekly plans include 6 bowls; monthly plans include 24 bowls. You can choose Paneer, Mexican, or a combo of both.

Is delivery included with meal plans?

Delivery is explicitly included with the weekly and monthly combo plans. Please confirm delivery terms for the Paneer-only and Mexican-only plans when ordering.

When should I place my order?

For lunch, order before 9:30 AM. For evening orders, order before 4:00 PM.

Do you offer customized packages?

Yes, custom packages are available. Contact AAHAR to discuss your requirements and pricing.
